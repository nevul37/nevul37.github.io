I have been recognized as a Microsoft Security Response Center (MSRC) Most Valuable Researcher, ranking in the Global Top 100 across seven consecutive quarters. My research has resulted in 52+ CVE disclosures and over $115,000 in bug bounties. Recently, my team SuperDiceCode achieved 3rd place at DEF CON 33 CTF, one of the most prestigious global CTF competitions.
